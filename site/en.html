About us

When industry can't stop, it calls us.

Design ·Build ·Rebuild ·Lift

Behind every mine that operates, every plant that produces and every lift completed on time, there is equipment that cannot fail. That's where we come in.

We are Óleo Hidráulica Especializada de Irapuato, known in the industry as Capricornio. For more than two decades we have supported Mexico's mining, steel, agribusiness and heavy industries with engineering, our own industrial infrastructure and a certified quality management system.
